      Your documents stay on this computer. They are read here, converted here,
      and written here — no file, and nothing out of a file, is ever sent
      anywhere, and there is no account, no server of ours, and nothing
      reporting back. This app reaches the internet in two situations only:
      setting itself up, which downloads Pandoc from GitHub and the PDF and
      spreadsheet readers from PyPI, and looking for a newer version, which
      asks GitHub what the newest release is. Both carry your computer's
      address and nothing else. The links above open GitHub, X or LinkedIn in
      your browser only when you click them.
